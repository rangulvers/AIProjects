Rubik's Cube Guides

Two printable, picture-based guides for kids (in German). Each move is drawn as a small cube with an arrow, so they can lay the sheet next to the cube and follow along.

Schicht für Schicht

The classic beginner method in 8 steps. Includes the move notation explained for kids, every case with a picture, and a one-page cheat sheet. 12 pages, A4.

PDFRead online

CFOP

The speedcuber method, with the beginner-friendly 2-look OLL and PLL. Cross, F2L cases, 10 OLL and 6 PLL algorithms, a 4-week practice plan and a cheat sheet. 12 pages, A4.

PDFRead online

How it was made

  • A small cube simulator (src/cube.py) turns every algorithm into the starting position shown in the pictures.
  • Every algorithm was checked in the simulator. For CFOP, all 288 possible last-layer states were solved with the two-look procedure.
  • The pages are plain HTML with inline SVG, rendered to PDF with Chromium.

The commands are in the project README in the repo (projects/rubiks-cube/README.md). Source is in src/.
